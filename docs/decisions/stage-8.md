# Stage 8 decision log: Own code and hardening

- **The own-code question is written in the background, not while the student waits.** The program is pasted before the viva starts, which queues an `own_code_question` job at high priority (a new `jobs.priority` puts it ahead of scoring, which nobody waits for). It is written while the student answers the first four questions: 1.5 to 2 seconds and about 800 tokens per question against real Groq. If it is not ready 45 seconds after the student reaches it, or the AI gives up after three attempts, a ready-made question about their program is used, so the viva never stalls. Both paths lock the session row first, so a late AI question can never land beside the fallback. Rejected: writing it when the student reaches the question, which on the free tier could keep 60 students waiting behind each other.
- **The program is untrusted input, and is only ever read.** It is stored as text (at most 20,000 characters), never compiled or run, and passed to the model inside tags it cannot close, with instructions to ignore anything inside it. Against real Groq, a program whose comment asked for "an easy question" got a question about its real bug, the missing overflow check in `push`. Live questions skip the teacher's approval, so each student gets only one, it belongs to their session (`questions.status = 'live'`), and the review page and report label it "generated live".
- **Every AI call leaves a record.** `ai_calls` holds one row per call to Groq, Gemini or Sarvam (purpose, model, outcome, time taken, time spent waiting for the rate limit, tokens, error), and each call also logs a line. `python -m app.ai.calls` sums them up by purpose. Writing the record can never fail the call it describes. Rejected: log lines alone, which cannot answer "how many scoring calls were refused today?".
- **The load test proves the submit path, not the AI's speed.** `scripts/load_test/run.py` signs in 60 real anonymous students who all join and start at once, then answer with random pauses; 20% double-click submit and 10% resend after a "lost" reply. Measured locally: 240 of 240 answers saved, none twice (42 double clicks, 31 resends), every viva completed, every answer with its score job, no failed requests, and a submit p95 of 0.07 s against the 2 s target (join p95 0.61 s, during the simultaneous start). It makes no AI calls: scoring throughput is limited by the provider's tier, not the code, and was measured in stage 5 (about 8 answers a minute on Groq's free tier). Not yet run: 180 students for three exams at once, because local Supabase allows 200 anonymous sign-ins an hour.
- **The scoring study is the number that shows the scoring can be trusted, and it needs a teacher.** `python -m app.modules.reports.study export` writes a random sample of AI-judged answers with no AI scores in the file; the teacher marks them blind; `compare` reports the share within 1 mark of the teacher (target 80% of 30 answers), exact agreement, mean difference and the largest gaps. Exact-match answers are left out, because code, not the AI, marked them. v1.0 waits for this result from real lab answers.
